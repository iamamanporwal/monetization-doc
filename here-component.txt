PART 0 — HOW TO USE THIS FILE
================================================================================

WHAT YOU ARE BUILDING
A working, clickable HTML prototype of HERE's two money dashboards:
  Product A — "NOW · Wallet"  : for the person spending credits (51 screens)
  Product B — "NOW · Control" : for the people deciding what credits cost (48)

You are NOT building the backend. Use the mock data in PART 5. State lives in
memory (a plain JS object) and resets on reload. That is fine and intended —
the point is to walk the user flow and feel whether it works.

BUILD ORDER — do it in this order, it matters
  1. PART 3  — drop the tokens into a stylesheet. Verify light AND dark.
  2. PART 6  — build all 28 components in isolation, on one "kitchen sink"
               page showing every variant and every state side by side.
  3. PART 7  — assemble screens from those components. Never draw a new
               element on a screen; if a screen needs something the library
               does not have, add it to the library first.
  4. PART 8  — wire the clickable flow.
  5. PART 10 — run the acceptance checklist before you call it done.

Do not skip step 2. Ninety-nine screens assembled from a library stay
consistent. Ninety-nine screens drawn one at a time drift by screen twenty.

NOTATION USED BELOW
  [C-01]        component ID — use these as CSS class names and file names
  U3.1 / D4.1   screen IDs (U = user product, D = decision-maker product)
  -->           "leads to" / "results in"
  REQUIRED      non-negotiable. If you cannot do it, stop and flag it.

PART 1 — PRODUCT CONTEXT
================================================================================

WHAT HERE IS
A social and creative platform. People work together inside shared worlds,
using AI agents, video capture and streaming, and a library of tools. Think of
it as a workspace that is also a game: groups form around a shared purpose and
do real work together.

THE VOCABULARY — use these exact words in all UI copy. Never substitute the
generic SaaS equivalent. This is the product's voice and it is not negotiable.

  JEM          The identity and sign-in layer. A person can hold a JEM identity
               without ever creating a HERE profile. Sign-in says "Continue
               with JEM", never "Sign in with SSO".

  Scircle      A shared world. A group of people with rooms, spaces, and a
               shared map of understanding. Plural: Scircles. This is the unit
               that holds a shared budget. NOT "workspace", NOT "team",
               NOT "organisation".

  Room         A conversation inside a Scircle. NOT "channel".

  Space        A perspective or slice that cuts across rooms.

  Guardian     An AI agent that does work. Either shared (pooled across many
               accounts) or dedicated (one guardian, one account).
               NOT "agent", NOT "assistant", NOT "bot".

  GO           The authorisation moment. Nothing paid ever runs without a GO.
               Used as a noun ("your first GO", "12 GOs this week") and as the
               label on the confirm button ("GO").

  Prime        An actively playing, paying member. Also the name of the entry
               paid tier.

  NOW          The command centre product where credits and billing live.

  Capture & stream   Recording, transcription, editing.
  Motion / Muse / Cerebral   Tools inside the platform.

TONE OF VOICE
  Plain, specific, unapologetic, short.
  WRITE:     "You weren't charged."
  NEVER:     "We apologise for any inconvenience this may have caused."
  WRITE:     "This failed on our end. The retry is free."
  NEVER:     "Something went wrong. Please try again later."
  WRITE:     "You'll run out on the 22nd at this rate."
  NEVER:     "Your balance may be insufficient for future operations."

  Active voice. A button says exactly what happens. A toast confirms in the
  same words the button used ("Publish" --> "Published").
  Errors say what broke, what it means for their money, and what to do.

PART 2 — THE MONEY MODEL
================================================================================
This is settled. Design to it. Do not reinvent any of it.

THE ANCHOR
  1 credit = $0.01 of list value.  100 credits = $1.00.
  Our target cost is $0.002 per credit (an 80% gross margin).
  Every price is set at 5x our cost, then rounded to a friendly integer.

  ONE EXCEPTION — INFRASTRUCTURE IS PRICED AT 3x, NOT 5x.
  Model and media actions hold 80%. Tier 8 hosting (compute, requests,
  egress, datastores) targets 65-70%, because Cloud Run, Replit and Emergent
  all publish their unit rates and a 5x markup on commodity compute is
  checkable. The UI must never imply one blended margin number.

MODEL TIERS — the multiplier tracks cost exactly, so margin is identical
whichever tier a person picks. This is why users can choose freely.

  TIER        MULTIPLIER   UNDERLYING      ONE RUN COSTS US   WE CHARGE
  Swift       x0.5         Haiku 4.5       $0.030             15 cr ($0.15)
  Standard    x1.0         Sonnet 5        $0.060             30 cr ($0.30)
  Deep        x2.5         Opus 5          $0.150             75 cr ($0.75)
  Cinematic   x5.0         Fable 5.1       $0.300             150 cr ($1.50)

  Standard is THE DEFAULT. Deep is a deliberate choice the user makes.
  ("One run" = 20,000 input tokens + 2,000 output tokens.)

PLANS
  TIER     PRICE/MO   CREDITS    EFFECTIVE $/CR   SCIRCLES   GUARDIAN
  Signal   $0         500        —                1          shared, low prio
  Prime    $39        5,000      $0.0078          3          shared pool
  Studio   $199       30,000     $0.0066          10         shared, priority
  World    $999       175,000    $0.0057          unlimited  priority + SSO

  ADD-ONS
  Dedicated Guardian   $899/month   one guardian, one account
  Motion embed         $2/site/month

  TOP-UP RATES (deliberately HIGHER than the plan allotment rate, so
  upgrading is always the better deal and the upgrade sells itself):
  Prime $0.0095/cr · Studio $0.0085/cr · World $0.0075/cr
  Minimum top-up: $10. Hard floor, never sell below $0.004/cr.

WALLET BUCKETS — spend order is ALWAYS soonest-expiring first
  1st  Promo       onboarding grant, campaign, apology     expires  7-30 days
  2nd  Plan        this month's allotment                  expires  cycle +30d grace
  3rd  Purchased   top-ups they paid cash for              expires  12 months
  4th  Overdraft   Studio and World only                   settles  next invoice

THE FIXED COST
  One guardian costs $500/month whether it does one job or ten thousand.
  Break-even is 17 accounts on a guardian. Target is 80 or more.
  Below 50 accounts is an amber alert. Below 17 is red — losing money.

THE FIVE RULES THE INTERFACE MUST NEVER BREAK — these are REQUIRED
  1. Nothing paid runs without an explicit GO that shows the cost first.
  2. A job that fails on our side is NEVER charged. The hold is voided.
     The retry is free. Say so in those words.
  3. Reading, browsing, replaying, joining and cancelling are always free
     and never metered.
  4. We settle the MEASURED amount, never the estimate. Charged is always
     less than or equal to quoted.
  5. Running out of credits drops someone into a SLOW LANE, never behind a
     locked door.
  6. Publishing is free; staying live is not - and the publish button itself
     states the monthly figure BEFORE the first deploy. A standing charge
     gets a RENEWAL GO every cycle, not a one-time GO at creation.
  7. SLEEP, NEVER DELETE. A surface that runs out of credits sleeps behind a
     wake page with its data kept 60 days. No surface ever goes dark without
     two warnings and a sleep state. Say all of this in those words.

CRITICAL THEMING RULE
  Declare every token on bare :root FIRST (that is the light theme).
  Then redefine ONLY the values under the two dark blocks. A colour whose
  only definition sits inside a dark block will not apply in the un-stamped
  state, and the page renders one theme's text on the other theme's ground.
  Always give <body> an explicit background from a token.

TYPOGRAPHY FOR THE MONEY SURFACES
  Body / UI      the --font system stack above
  Numbers        a monospace with TABULAR NUMERALS. REQUIRED on every credit

                 ledger row. Use ui-monospace / SF Mono / Menlo, or load

  REQUIRED: money never shifts layout as it changes. Reserve the width.
  A balance ticking from 4,970 to 4,940 must not move a single pixel of

SPACING
  4px grid. Card padding 20px (--pad). Gaps 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24.

SEMANTIC COLOUR MAPPING for money states — use these consistently
  healthy / kept / success / margin good   --green  on --green-wash
  caution / warn / approaching a limit     --amber  on --amber-wash

  credit / balance / the meter itself      --ok (teal) — deliberately NOT the

  · Visible keyboard focus on everything interactive: box-shadow: var(--ring)
  · Respect @media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) — disable transitions
  · Every form control gets a stable id and a real <label>

PART 4 — THE DATA MODEL
================================================================================
The shapes your components read. Build your mock store to match these exactly,
so swapping in a real API later is a one-file change.

THE CARDINAL RULE: A BALANCE IS NOT A COLUMN.
It is a SUM over an append-only ledger. Never store a mutable balance field.
Every component that shows a balance derives it. This is what makes the
numbers trustworthy and the history explainable.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ledger_entry            // append-only. Never updated. Never deleted.
  id                    string    "le_01H..."
  account_id            string
  scircle_id            string?   null for personal spend
  surface_id            string?   NEW in 2.0. Set on every standing row and
                                  on hosting overage, so a user can ask
                                  "what did this app cost me" and get an
                                  answer without arithmetic.
  type                  enum      grant | spend | refund | expiry | adjustment
                                  | standing        <-- NEW in 2.0. A
                                  recurring Tier 8 charge settled in arrears
                                  at cycle end. Never has a hold; never has
                                  a GO card at spend time (it had a renewal
                                  GO earlier in the cycle). MUST be
                                  filterable separately, because standing
                                  charges would otherwise inflate the
                                  weekly-burn North Star without telling you
                                  anything about engagement.

DERIVED VALUES — compute, never store
  balance              = SUM(ledger_entry.delta) for the account
  available            = balance - SUM(open holds)
  burn_rate_per_day    = SUM(spend deltas last 7 days) / 7
      NOTE: exclude `standing` rows from this sum. They are recurring, not
      daily behaviour, and folding them in makes the sparkline lie.

  monthly_standing_commitment          <-- NEW in 2.0
      = SUM(surface.credits_monthly) for surfaces where included_in_plan
        is false and state = 'live'
      What this account owes every cycle before doing a single thing.

  runway_date                          <-- CORRECTED in 2.0
      = today + (available - remaining_standing_this_cycle)
                / burn_rate_per_day
      where remaining_standing_this_cycle prorates
      monthly_standing_commitment over the days left in the cycle.

      *** SPEC 1.0's RUNWAY FORMULA WAS WRONG FOR ANYONE HOSTING ANYTHING.
      It divided the balance by a 7-day burn rate and ignored the recurring
      monthly draw entirely, so it OVERSTATED runway for exactly the users
      most likely to be surprised by a charge. Fix this before U3.1 ships. ***
  burn_depth           = credits_burned_this_cycle / credits_granted_this_cycle
  accounts_per_guardian (APG)  = guardian.accounts_served.length
  guardian_coverage_ratio (GCR)= attributed_gross_profit / 500
  surface_margin               = (credits_billed * 0.01 - cogs_actual_usd)
                                 / (credits_billed * 0.01)
      Target >= 65%. Warn < 50%. Act < 35%. The hosting analogue of GCR.

IDEMPOTENCY — this one bites in production, model it in the prototype
  Every spend carries a client-generated key, ONE PER USER ACTION, reused
  across retries, with a unique index behind it. Without this, a flaky
  connection double-charges someone — and a double charge is the exact event
  that destroys the trust all fifty user screens exist to create.

[C-01] CREDIT VALUE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  The atom. Renders a credit amount consistently everywhere it appears. If
  every credit number in the product goes through this component, they can
  never disagree in format, alignment or colour.

ANATOMY
  [value] [unit label] [optional secondary currency] [optional state marker]

PROPS
  amount        integer, signed
  variant       plain | withCurrency | delta | held | estimate
  size          sm (13px) | md (15px) | lg (22px) | xl (34px)
  rate          number — the account's effective $/credit, for withCurrency
  band          number? — for estimate variant, the +/- range

VARIANTS
  plain         "5,985 cr"
  withCurrency  "5,985 cr" with "$59.85" beneath or beside in --ink-3
  delta         "-30 cr" in --ink / "+75 cr" in --green. Sign ALWAYS shown.
  held          "42 cr held" in --amber with a dotted underline
  estimate      "~35 cr" with "30-42" as the band, in --ink-2

STATES
  default · muted (inside a disabled row) · emphasised (the one number the
  screen is about)

RULES — REQUIRED
  · font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums  ALWAYS
  · Thousands separators always. "5,985" never "5985".
  · Never a decimal on credits. Credits are integers. Currency gets 2dp.
  · The unit is "cr" in compact contexts, "credits" when there is room.
  · Reserve width so the number changing does not reflow anything.
    Use a min-width in ch units sized to the largest plausible value.

COPY
  Never say "tokens", "points", "coins" or "units". Always "credits" / "cr".

HTML SKETCH
  <span class="c-credit c-credit--withCurrency c-credit--lg">
    <span class="c-credit__value">5,985</span>
    <span class="c-credit__unit">cr</span>
    <span class="c-credit__fiat">$59.85</span>
  </span>

[C-02] FUEL GAUGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  Shows remaining balance as a proportion, not just a number. The research on
  credit products is clear: a visible usage bar makes a limit feel transparent
  rather than punitive. This is the single most-looked-at element in Product A.

ANATOMY
  [track] [fill] [optional healthy band overlay] [optional cap marker]
  [center label: big number + unit] [sub label: context]

PROPS
  remaining     integer
  total         integer
  variant       ring | bar | inline
  band          [lo, hi]?  — the healthy zone, e.g. [0.6, 0.8] for burn depth
  capMarker     number?    — a user-set cap, drawn as a tick
  tone          auto | ok | warn | risk

VARIANTS
  ring     180px diameter, 14px stroke. Used on U3.1 wallet home.
           Center holds a C-01 at size xl, plus a sub-label.
  bar      full-width, 10px tall, radius 5px. Used in lists and cards.
  inline   60px wide, 6px tall. Sits beside a label in a table row.

TONE LOGIC (when tone is auto)
  remaining/total > 0.40   --ok      (teal)
  0.15 - 0.40              --amber
  < 0.15                   risk red
  = 0                      --ink-3, and the slow-lane state applies (see C-09)

STATES
  default · low (under 15%) · empty (0) · over-cap · loading (skeleton arc,

COPY
  Center:    "5,985"  /  sub: "credits left"
  Low:       "340"    /  sub: "credits left · about 1 day"
  Empty:     "0"      /  sub: "you're in the slow lane"

BEHAVIOUR
  Animates on change over 400ms with --eo easing. Under
  prefers-reduced-motion, snaps.

ACCESSIBILITY
  role="meter" with aria-valuenow / aria-valuemin / aria-valuemax and an

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[C-05] GO CARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  The authorisation moment. Nothing paid ever runs without one. It answers, in
  one glance: what is about to happen, which model will do it, what it will
  cost, and what the worst case is.

  This component is why users trust HERE. The complaint it is designed against,
  which appears in every credit-product teardown, is: "I can't tell what an
  action costs until after it runs."

THREE VARIANTS — genuinely different components sharing a visual family

  ===== C-05a  INSTANT =====
  For sub-second actions: a room turn, a Muse tool call, a page extraction.
  REQUIRED: this variant does NOT interrupt. There is no dialog and no
  confirm. Interrupting someone to approve 3 credits is worse than the charge.
  It renders as a persistent chip showing the session running total.

  ANATOMY   [tiny meter icon] [session total] [caret to open a popover]
  POPOVER   last 8 actions with per-action cost, session total, a link to
            the full ledger, and a "pause metered actions" switch
  COPY      chip:    "42 cr this session"
            popover title: "This session"
            popover row:   "Room turn · Standard · 6 cr"
            switch:  "Pause metered actions"
  POSITION  bottom-right, above the fold, never covering primary content
  STATE     idle · counting (number ticks, aria-live polite) · paused

  ===== C-05b  JOB =====  the canonical GO card
  For anything measurable that takes seconds to minutes: transcribe, render,
  a guardian run, a world recompute.

  ANATOMY, top to bottom
    1  Eyebrow      tool name + version, small mono, --ink-3
                    "capture.transcribe v3"
    2  Headline     what will happen, in the user's words, 20px semibold
                    "Transcribe this recording"
    3  Detail row   the inputs, plain
                    "Standup — 18 min 24 s"
    4  Model picker [C-06] — four tiers, live cost per tier, one recommended
    5  Estimate     [C-01 variant=estimate] big, with the band
                    "~55 cr"  and beneath: "between 50 and 62"
    6  Cap row      a stepper: "Stop at 80 cr" with - / + and a hint
                    "We'll stop rather than go past this."
    7  Balance line "You have 5,985 cr. After this, about 5,930."
    8  Remember     checkbox: "Don't ask again under 100 cr for this tool"
    9  Actions      [Cancel] [GO]

  LAYOUT      modal on desktop (max-width 440px), bottom sheet on mobile.
  CTA         primary "GO" in --brand-solid with white text.
  REQUIRED    Cancel is a real button of equal visual weight, never a small
              grey link. Escape closes. Clicking the backdrop closes.
              There is NO dark pattern anywhere in this component.

  STATES
    default · model-changed (estimate recalculates with a 200ms count
    animation) · over-balance (see C-09, the card hands off) ·
    over-cap (the estimate exceeds the cap: cap row turns --amber, GO is
    still enabled but labelled "GO — will stop at 80 cr") ·
    approval-required (the Scircle threshold is exceeded: GO becomes
    "Request approval" and a note names the approver) ·
    loading-estimate (skeleton on the estimate, GO disabled)

  ===== C-05c  STANDING =====
  For anything continuous or recurring: live production, a scheduled guardian,
  residual marketing from a broadcast.

  REQUIRED: a standing job cannot be authorised without all three of a budget,
  an expiry and a stop condition. It stops by default rather than continuing
  by default. This is the component that prevents the classic credit-product
  disaster of a forgotten job spending forever.

  ANATOMY adds to C-05b
    Budget      "Spend up to [ 2,000 ] cr"
    Expiry      "Stop on [ 4 Oct ]" (date picker, max 90 days out)
    Stop when   checklist, at least one REQUIRED:
                [x] the budget is used up
                [x] the date passes
                [ ] the source broadcast ends
                [ ] I stop it manually
    Preview     "At the current rate that's about 6 days."
  ACTIONS       [Cancel] [Start]   — the verb is "Start", not "GO"

PROPS (all variants)
  tool, version, headline, detail, estimate, band[lo,hi], cap, modelTier,
  modelOptions[], balance, scircle, approvalThreshold, rememberEligible

BEHAVIOUR
  · Changing the model tier recalculates the estimate live, in front of them.
  · The cap defaults to ceil(estimate * 1.5), rounded to something friendly.
  · On GO: create a hold of estimate * 1.2, close the card, show C-07.
  · On Cancel: nothing is created. No ledger row. No charge. Ever.

ACCESSIBILITY
  role="dialog" aria-modal="true", focus trapped, focus starts on the
  headline (not the GO button — never make Enter an accidental purchase),
  Escape closes, focus returns to the trigger.

HTML SKETCH (C-05b)
  <div class="c-go c-go--job" role="dialog" aria-modal="true"

[C-06] MODEL TIER PICKER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  Lets a person choose how much intelligence to spend, and shows the cost
  changing as they choose. The multipliers hold our margin constant, so free
  choice costs us nothing — which means we can make this genuinely open, and
  that openness is a feature competitors cannot easily copy.

ANATOMY
  Four segments in a row (stacked on mobile), each:
    [tier name] [what it's good for, 3-4 words] [cost for THIS job]
  Plus one "recommended" marker on the tier that fits the job.

PROPS
  tiers[]   { id, name, multiplier, costForThisJob, blurb }
  selected, recommended, disabled[]

CONTENT — exact copy
  Swift      "quick, good enough"     x0.5
  Standard   "the everyday choice"    x1     <- default, usually recommended
  Deep       "when it really matters" x2.5
  Cinematic  "the best we have"       x5

STATES
  default · selected (--brand-solid border 2px, --brand-wash fill) ·
  recommended (a small "recommended" chip; if it is also selected, both show) ·
  hover · focus-visible · disabled (tier not in their plan — greyed, with a
  lock icon and tooltip "Studio and above") · price-loading (dash, not zero)

BEHAVIOUR
  Selecting recalculates the parent estimate immediately. Never require a
  separate confirm within the picker.

RULES
  REQUIRED: show the cost for THIS SPECIFIC JOB on each tier, not the
  multiplier alone. "75 cr" is actionable; "x2.5" is homework.
  Show the multiplier as secondary information for people who want it.

ACCESSIBILITY
  role="radiogroup" with role="radio" children, arrow-key navigation.

[C-07] LIVE METER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  Shows a job actually running and consuming, against the hold. Turns waiting
  into watching, and makes the cap feel real.

ANATOMY
  [tool + headline] [progress bar against the cap] [units counted so far]
  [credits so far] [elapsed] [Cancel]

PROPS
  jobId, headline, unitsDone, unitsEstimate, unitLabel, creditsSoFar, cap,
  startedAt

STATES
  starting     "Starting…" with an indeterminate bar
  counting     bar advances, numbers tick
  near-cap     at 80% of cap: bar turns --amber, note "approaching your 80 cr cap"
  at-cap       bar --amber full, job stops itself, hands to C-08 as a partial
  cancelling   bar freezes, "Stopping… you'll only pay for what we measured"
  done         hands off to C-08

COPY
  "14.2 of about 18 audio minutes · 43 cr so far · 0:52 elapsed"
  Cancel button: "Stop"
  On stop:  "Stopped. You paid for the 14.2 minutes we measured — 43 cr."

BEHAVIOUR
  REQUIRED: Cancel actually cancels, and settles ONLY what was measured.
  A cancel that still charges the full estimate is the fastest way to lose
  a user permanently.

ACCESSIBILITY
  aria-live="polite" on the credit figure, but throttle announcements to
  once every 5 seconds — a screen reader reading every tick is unusable.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[C-08] SETTLE RECEIPT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  The moment trust is either built or lost. Shows that we charged LESS than we
  quoted, and returns the difference in front of them.

ANATOMY
  [outcome marker] [what ran] [held --> used --> returned]
  [new balance] [link to the full receipt]

PROPS
  outcome    completed | partial | failed
  held, used, returned, balanceAfter, jobId, headline

VARIANTS
  toast   6s auto-dismiss, bottom-right, for routine completions
  panel   inline in the tool surface, stays until dismissed
  page    the full receipt (screen U3.5)

STATES BY OUTCOME — the copy is the component here, get it exactly right

  completed   marker: --green check
              "Done. We held 60, it used 55. You keep 5."
              sub: "Balance 5,930 cr"

  partial     marker: --amber
              "Stopped at your cap. You paid for the 14.2 minutes we
               measured — 43 cr. The rest of the hold is back."

  failed      marker: risk red, but the COPY is reassuring not alarming
              "This failed on our end. You weren't charged.
               The 75 cr hold is back in your balance."
              action: [Try again — free]

RULES — REQUIRED
  · Always state all three numbers: held, used, returned.
  · Never show only the charge. "55 cr" alone is a worse experience than
    "held 60, used 55, kept 5", even though it is the same money.
  · The failed variant NEVER uses apologetic corporate language and NEVER
    routes to a support form as the primary action. The primary action is
    a free retry.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[C-09] INSUFFICIENT CREDITS SHEET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  The revenue moment, handled honestly. Appears when available balance is
  below estimate x 1.2.

  REQUIRED: this sheet NEVER charges automatically. Auto-recharge fires only
  if the user turned it on themselves, and when it does, it says so plainly
  before running.

ANATOMY
  [headline] [the gap, stated as a number] [four options as cards] [Cancel]

THE FOUR OPTIONS — always all four, always this order
  1  Top up          "Add 2,500 cr — $25"       --> C-15 checkout
     sub: "at $0.0095 each on Prime"
     REQUIRED: beside it, the honest comparison —
     "On Studio these would cost $0.0085. You'd save $2.50 a month."
  2  Use Swift       "Run it on Swift instead — 28 cr"
     sub: "quicker, good enough for most transcripts"
  3  Trim the scope  "Just the first 10 minutes — 30 cr"
     sub: only shown when the tool supports partial scope
  4  Cancel          plain button, no styling tricks

COPY
  Headline: "You're 18 credits short."
  Sub:      "This needs about 55 cr. You have 37 available."
  (If a hold is the reason:) "42 cr of yours are held by a job still running."

STATES
  default · slow-lane-available (adds a fifth option: "Use the slow lane —
  free, but you'll wait" with the current queue position) ·
  auto-recharge-armed (shows "Auto-recharge will add 2,500 cr ($25) — you
  turned this on" with a confirm, never a silent charge) ·
  scircle-pool-available ("Harbour has 8,420 cr — spend from the pool?"
  with the payer picker C-20)

THE SLOW LANE — this is a deliberate steal from Midjourney's Relax Mode and
it is one of the strongest ideas in the product. Running out must not stop
someone dead. They drop to Swift at low queue priority, free, with a visible
"you're in the slow lane" state and a one-tap way out. Hitting zero should
feel like a speed limit, not a locked door.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[C-16] DUNNING BANNER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  Communicates a failed payment without punishing the user. The governing
  principle is DEGRADE, NEVER DELETE.

FOUR STAGES — the copy IS the component

  stage 1 · retrying (days 1-7) · --amber banner, full access retained
    "Your card ending 4142 was declined. We'll try again on Tuesday."
    [Update payment method]
    Note: nothing is taken away while we are still trying.

  stage 2 · grace (days 7-14) · --amber, persistent, dismissible per session
    "You're on the free lane while we sort your payment out."
    sub: "Reading, replay and export all still work. Paid jobs are paused
          and your guardians are asleep. Nothing has been deleted."
    [Update payment method]

  stage 3 · suspended (days 14-30) · risk red, not dismissible
    "Your account is read-only. Nothing has been deleted."
    sub: "Harbour and Ridge are still working for everyone else in them."
    [Update payment method] [Export everything]

  stage 4 · resolved · --green, auto-dismiss after 8s
    "You're back. Everything that was paused is running again."

RULES — REQUIRED
  · One person's card failing must NEVER break a shared Scircle for the
    other members. Say so explicitly at stage 3.
  · Never delete anything before day 30. After cancellation, a 60-day
    export window.
  · No guilt language at any stage.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[C-17] PAYWALL SHEET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  Converts a free user. Three trigger variants that convert differently — you
  will want to measure which, so build all three as distinct entry contexts
  of one component.

THREE TRIGGERS
  a · exhausted      allotment used up
      "You've used September's 500 credits."
      Primary: the slow lane is offered FIRST, upgrade second. REQUIRED —
      leading with the paywall at the moment of exhaustion reads as a
      hostage situation; leading with the free fallback reads as generous,
      and converts better over a 30-day window.

  b · feature-gated  they reached for a tier-locked tool
      "Cerebral is on Studio and above."
      Shows exactly what the tool does and what it would cost per use.

  c · value-moment   right after something worked well
      "That render came out well. Three more like it are on Prime."
      The softest and highest-converting trigger. Fires at most once a week.

ANATOMY
  [context line: why you are seeing this] [1-2 plan cards, not all four]
  [one secondary option] [dismiss]

RULES — REQUIRED
  Dismiss is always available and always obvious. A paywall you cannot
  close is the single most-screenshotted dark pattern on the internet.
  Never show all four plans in a paywall — show the one that fits plus one
  step up. The full comparison lives on U5.2.

[C-27] STANDING METER CARD                                     [NEW in 2.0]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE

  (C-05) answers "what will this cost me now." This answers "what is this
  costing me forever," which is the question a recurring charge actually
  raises and the one v1 of the product had no place to answer.

ANATOMY
  surface name + its URL (the URL is the point — make it a link)
  class chip           static | live app | always-on
  state chip           live · sleeping · stopped · deploying
  the monthly number   large, in credits AND fiat, per C-01
  "included in your plan" badge when included_in_plan is true, and then the
    monthly number renders as 0 with the list price struck through — people
    need to see the value of what they are not being charged for
  this cycle so far  a thin meter against budget_cap
  built by <guardian> — every surface has an author, show it
  actions              [Change budget] [Sleep it] [Open]

VARIANTS
  included (bills zero) · billing · approaching cap · over cap ·
  sleeping · warned-once · warned-twice

STATES TO BUILD
  All seven. The warned-twice state is the one that matters: it must say
  what happens next and when, in a date, not in a duration.

RULES
  REQUIRED: never show a monthly cost without the date it next settles.
  REQUIRED: the sleeping variant says the data is kept and for how long, in
  those words. A user reading this screen is deciding whether to trust us

  REQUIRED: "Sleep it" is always available and never buried. Giving someone
  a one-tap way to stop a recurring charge is what makes the recurring
  charge acceptable in the first place.

   Wake it any time; nothing was lost."

[C-28] SLEEP / WAKE SHEET                                      [NEW in 2.0]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
  The out-of-credits moment for something that is LIVE. C-09 (insufficient
  credits) handles a job that will not start. This handles a thing already
  running that we are about to stop — a strictly worse moment, because the
  user has an audience.

ANATOMY
  what is about to happen, with a date
  what it costs to keep it running    (credits, fiat, per cycle)
  what happens if they do nothing     — sleeps, does NOT delete
  how long the data is kept           — 60 days, stated as a date
  choices, in this order:
    [Top up and keep it live]  [Move to a smaller plan]  [Let it sleep]
  and a quiet line: "Sleeping is free. You can wake it in one tap."

VARIANTS
  warning-one (14 days out) · warning-two (3 days out) · sleeping-now ·
  wake-confirm

RULES
  REQUIRED: lead with the free fallback, not the payment. A page that leads
  with "pay or lose it" reads as a hostage situation; leading with "it will
  sleep safely, here is how to keep it awake" reads as generous and
  converts better. This mirrors the existing rule on C-17.
  REQUIRED: never use the word "delete" anywhere in this component, in any
  state. We do not delete a live surface to collect a debt, and the copy is
  where that promise is actually kept.
  REQUIRED: the wake-confirm variant confirms the URL is unchanged. That is
  the specific fear.

PART 9 — BUILD INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================

STACK
  Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JS is entirely sufficient and is what the
  existing HERE app uses (no build step, hash routing, one file per screen).
  If you prefer React, fine — but keep the class names from this document so
  the CSS is portable back into the real app.

FILE STRUCTURE — suggested
  /money-proto
    index.html            hash-routed shell + nav between screens
    tokens.css            PART 3
    components.css        one section per component, in C-xx order
    screens.css           layout only; never colours or type
    store.js              PART 5 mock data + the derived getters
    components.js         one factory per component, exported by ID
    screens/*.js          one file per screen, assembling components
    kitchen-sink.html     every component, every variant, every state

CONVENTIONS
  · Class naming: c-<component> / c-<component>__<part> /
    c-<component>--<variant>. Matches the sketches above.
  · Never hardcode a colour. Every colour is a var(--token).
  · Never hardcode a credit number in markup. It comes from the store.
  · All money formatting goes through one function, fmtCredits(n) and
    fmtMoney(n). One place to fix, one place to test.
  · Derive balances. Do not store them. See PART 4.

THE THEME SWITCH
  Support all three states: explicit light, explicit dark, and system
  default (no attribute). Put a toggle in the prototype shell so a reviewer
  can flip it — half the bugs in a money UI are dark-mode contrast bugs and
  you will not find them otherwise.

WHAT TO FAKE AND WHAT NOT TO
  FAKE:      network latency (300-900ms), job execution (a 3-5s timer),
             payment processing, email sends
  DO NOT FAKE: the ledger. Build it properly as an append-only array with
             derived balances, holds that actually reserve, and settlement
             that actually releases the difference. The whole product rests
             on this behaving correctly, and if you fake it the prototype
             will not tell you what you need to know.

DELIBERATELY BUILD ONE OF EACH EDGE CASE INTO THE DEMO
  · a job that fails and refunds (already in the mock ledger)
  · a bucket expiring in 3 days
  · an account below the healthy burn-depth band
  · a guardian below the alert floor
  · a pending hold that is excluded from the balance
  These are the cases that break real implementations. Having them in the
  prototype from hour one means they get designed, not discovered.

PART 10 — ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST
================================================================================
Run this before calling it done. Every line is a REQUIRED behaviour from the
five rules in PART 2.

TRUST — the five rules
  [ ] No paid action can start without a GO card showing the cost first
  [ ] A failed job charges zero. Verify the balance is numerically unchanged.
  [ ] The retry after a failure is free and labelled as free
  [ ] Reading, browsing, replay, join and cancel never produce a ledger row
  [ ] Settle always charges <= the quote. Never once more.
  [ ] Cancelling a GO card creates nothing — no hold, no row, no charge
  [ ] Stopping a running job charges only the measured units
  [ ] Zero balance reaches the slow lane, not a dead end
  [ ] Nothing auto-charges unless auto-recharge was switched on by the user,
      and even then it announces itself before charging

CORRECTNESS
  [ ] Balance is derived from the ledger everywhere; no stored balance field
  [ ] Holds reduce available but not balance, and are shown separately
  [ ] Buckets spend soonest-expiry first, and the UI states that they do
  [ ] Every ledger row shows tool, model, units, rate version
  [ ] An expired bucket produces a visible expiry row, not a silent decrease
  [ ] Numbers agree across screens: wallet, ledger, invoice and receipt all
      show the same figure for the same event

INTERFACE
  [ ] Every number uses tabular numerals
  [ ] No layout shift when a number changes
  [ ] All nine states exist for every screen built
  [ ] Light and dark both pass contrast, checked on real text not just chips
  [ ] Works at 390px with no horizontal scroll
  [ ] Keyboard: full traverse, visible focus, Escape closes every modal
  [ ] prefers-reduced-motion disables the animations
  [ ] No empty state says "no data" — each one says what to do next

COPY
  [ ] The vocabulary is used correctly: JEM, Scircle, Room, Guardian, GO,
      Prime, NOW. No "workspace", "agent", "team" or "organisation".
  [ ] No apologetic error copy anywhere
  [ ] Cancel is as visually available as confirm on every destructive or
      spending action
  [ ] No paywall can trap a user

THE TWO TESTS THAT MATTER MOST
  [ ] A person can answer "what did I spend and why" in ten seconds,
      starting from the wallet, without asking anyone
  [ ] An operator can find the one account or guardian that needs them
      today, from D1.1, in under three clicks


================================================================================
OPEN QUESTIONS — flag these, do not guess
================================================================================
These are unresolved at the time of writing. Build with the stated assumption
and mark the spot so it is easy to change later.

1  GUARDIAN CONCURRENCY. Everything assumes one shared guardian serves many
   accounts, with a practical ceiling near 120. If a guardian is actually
   one-to-one with a customer, then accounts-per-guardian collapses to 1 and
   every price shown on the plan screens is wrong. C-25 and D4.x depend on
   this most.  ASSUMPTION: many accounts per guardian.

2  REAL COGS. The cost figures come from published model list prices
   (Haiku $1/$5, Sonnet $2/$10, Opus $5/$25, Fable $10/$50 per million
   tokens) and market rates for media and storage. They have not been
   reconciled against real invoices. ASSUMPTION: $0.002 cost per credit.

3  THE NOW SHELL. These are page bodies. It is not settled whether they sit
   inside the existing HERE app shell (sidebar, app bar, drawer, command
   palette) or need their own. ASSUMPTION: they sit inside the existing
   shell and do not redesign it.

4  OPERATOR ROLES. Five roles are specified. It may be that only founder and
   support exist today. ASSUMPTION: build founder + support views first, and
   leave the other three role filters stubbed.

5  PAYMENT GATEWAY. Copy assumes a hosted-fields gateway (Paddle as merchant
   of record, or Stripe). The checkout iframe and the tax line differ
   slightly between them. ASSUMPTION: merchant-of-record, tax inclusive in
   the displayed total for EU/UK, exclusive for US.
