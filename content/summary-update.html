The one big decision

Two machines, don't mix them

Credits  ·  Billing

Credits = the trust interface users see (predictable, previewed, nothing charged for failure).

Billing = the finance machine (revenue, margin, tax) that users barely see.

The fixed-cost problem — now doubled

Two pillars, not one

The original doc was built around one fixed cost, the $500/month guardian. With hosting, there are two: guardians and the servers keeping users' published apps alive.

Both run whether anyone's active or not — so pure pay-as-you-go still can't carry them. The two-part tariff (subscription floor + metered credits) now covers two shapes of fixed cost.

The trap, with a new exception

Never let credit prices float with your costs. But the "every price = 5× cost (80% margin)" rule now applies only to model and media actions.

Infrastructure targets 3× (65–70% margin) — because Replit, Emergent and Cloud Run publish their prices, and a 5× markup on commodity compute is checkable, and it gets checked.

Billing and the fixed costs

Money in ≠ revenue, still. Cash sits as a liability; it becomes revenue as credits burn. Standing hosting charges bill monthly in arrears against the wallet.

Capacity management now covers two fleets

Guardians (the four existing rules) plus surfaces — three new margin levers:

Lever · surfaces 1

Scale-to-zero by default

Nothing idles on our dime unless someone paid for always-on.

Lever · surfaces 2

Auto-sleep idle surfaces

Idle time is pure cost with no revenue attached.

Lever · surfaces 3

Cold-storage sleeping app images

Sleeping surfaces cost storage, not compute.

The dunning ladder extends to hosting Out of credits → warn → warn → sleep. Never purge, never hold data hostage.

Unchanged

  • Paddle → Stripe.
  • Fixed local price points.
  • Credits legally framed as expiring usage rights (never money).
  • $10 top-up floor.
Retired

The old “one fixed cost” sentence is retired — the doc now explicitly carries guardian + hosting as the two fixed pillars the subscription floor exists to cover.
